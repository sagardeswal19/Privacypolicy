Rescue the King: Privacy Policy

Published by Aarushi Chawla. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Rescue the King is a puzzle game for Android. This page explains, in plain words, what information the game uses, why, and what choices you have. If anything here is unclear, write to us at Chwlaaaru@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no account of its own. We do not ask for your email, phone number or address.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, it is also saved to your Google account so you can move to a new phone.
  • The game shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to count how the game is used and to find crashes.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Ads and your choices

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Some ads you choose to watch in return for a reward. Others appear between levels. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent in a form from Google, and if you are in certain US states it offers the choices that the law there requires. You can change your mind at any time with the Ad privacy choices link on the home screen (it shows where the law requires it).

On your Android phone you can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation: Settings, Google, Ads.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information goes only to the Google services listed above, which process it for us and for their own purposes as described in their policies. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep information

Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Firebase keeps usage data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. Your Play Games saved game stays in your Google account until you delete it in the Play Games settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to see, correct, delete or move your information, to object to some uses, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email Chwlaaaru@gmail.com. Because the game has no account, we may ask you for the installation ID or other details we need to find your data. You can also use the Google tools mentioned above to manage your Google data directly.

Children

Rescue the King is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

We use the standard protections of the Google services above. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game does with information, we will update this page and the date at the top. Please check it from time to time.
